# Goals and non-goals

| | What |
|---|---|
| Goal | You name a song, and you get lessons for it in a few minutes. |
| Goal | The app reads each key you press on the Yamaha, with no felt delay. |
| Goal | Each lesson starts easy: one hand, slow, few bars. It gets harder only when you play it well. |
| Goal | A short basics course for a near beginner: middle C, finger numbers, simple reading. |
| Goal | It runs on iPad. It also runs on iPhone, with a smaller view. |
| Non-goal | The App Store, accounts or other users. The app is for one person. |
| Non-goal | Sharing or selling song files. Songs stay on your own device, for your own practice. |
| Non-goal | Teaching music theory in depth, or singing. |
| Non-goal | A perfect copy of the record. A song is made easy on purpose. |
| Non-goal | Feedback from the microphone. The app reads keys over the cable only. |

How we know it worked: you ask for three songs you like. Within four weeks,
you play the tune of one of them with the right hand, from start to end.
