# Summary

D5 is a short course that comes before the first song. It has seven
lessons. Each lesson teaches one idea in two or three short paragraphs.
Then you play two or three exercises in wait mode, the same mode as the
practice screen of D4.

A lesson opens when you pass each exercise of the lesson before it. To pass,
you get 9 of 10 notes right. From lesson 5, time counts too: 1 note in 10
can be late, and no more. Your passes stay on the iPad.

A worked example:

| Step | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Arjun opens "Basics course" for the first time | Lesson 1 is open. Lessons 2 to 7 show a lock |
| Read | Lesson 2, "Right hand in C position" | Three short paragraphs: finger numbers, the C position, curved fingers |
| Play | Exercise 2.2, "Up and down": C D E F G F E D C, fingers 1 to 5 and back. Arjun presses one wrong key on the 4th note | The key shows red. The screen waits for F4 again |
| Score | 9 notes, 8 right | "Not yet". 89 percent is less than 90 percent |
| Again | Arjun plays it again with no wrong key | "Passed". Exercise 2.2 gets a tick, and the tick stays after the app closes |
| Gate | Arjun passes 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3 | Lesson 3 opens |
